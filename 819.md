#### **Food Integrity at WHUT[Xuehai 819]**
##### **What is Food Integrity?**
>*Adapted from:* [Academic Integrity at MIT – A Handbook for Students](https://integrity.mit.edu/)

Fundamental to the dining you do around [Lab] is an expectation that you will make choices that reflect integrity and responsible behavior.

The lab will ask much of you. Occasionally, you may feel overwhelmed by the amount of work in front of you. You may be short of time, running several experiments that finish at the same hour, or preparing for a defense. Hunger at that point can be intense. The Ordering Under Pressure page lists restaurants with short waiting times and few ways to go wrong. However, no matter how hungry or rushed you are, this Guide expects you to approach every recommendation with honesty and integrity.

Honesty is the foundation of a good recommendation. Whether you are recommending a restaurant, a dish, or a delivery order, avoid fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, undisclosed conflicts of interest, or helping others do any of these. Follow this advice:

1. **Eat there first.** Recommend only what you have personally eaten.
2. **Report what happened.** Price, spice level, portion size and waiting time are measurements. Do not round them in the restaurant's favor.
3. **Credit your sources.** If a colleague told you about the place, write their name on the card, not yours.
4. **Disclose relationships.** If you are related to the owner, or used to work there, say so in the declaration field. (To avoid favoritism and corrupt practices)
5. **Recommend again only with new evidence.** A second visit counts. The same opinion repeated does not.
6. **When unsure, ask.** If you are not certain a place qualifies, put it on the To Be Tried list instead of the main list.

**Misrepresentation of Recommendations**

| Do                                                              | Don’t                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trust the value of your own taste.                              | Don’t copy someone else’s review and post it as your own.                                                |
| Eat there yourself and credit the person who told you about it. | Don’t copy ideas, ratings, photos or exact wording without citing your source.                           |
| Write your own honest review based on your own meal.            | Don’t purchase reviews, ask a friend to write a review for you, or let AI invent a visit you never made. |


**Faking the Food Experience**

|Do|Don’t|
|---|---|
|Demonstrate your own dining experience.|Don’t copy ratings from another student; don’t ask another student to eat there for you. Don’t fabricate visits. Don’t use photos from the internet and claim you took them.|
|Accept corrections from the Guide maintainers as part of the review process.|Don’t alter a previous review after it has been graded and submit it for re-grading.|
|Do original reviews for each restaurant.|Don’t submit reviews or photos that have been done for a previous guide, class, or social media post.|


**Facilitating Food Misinformation**

| Do                                                                     | Don’t                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Showcase your own honest recommendations.                              | Don’t allow another student to copy your review or rating. Don’t eat for another student or write their recommendation for them. |
| Help others by sharing accurate wait times, prices, and portion sizes. | Don’t help others fake a visit, hide a conflict of interest, or inflate a restaurant’s score.                                    |
| Encourage responsible eating and honest reporting.                     | Don’t encourage others to review a place they have never tried.                                                                  |

#### 推荐 Recommendations

>**如何写一条 Qualified 的推荐**
  下述为我们的推荐写法，仅供参考：
  一条推荐（餐馆、外卖都可以）通常包括下述内容：店名、位置或平台、人均价格、推荐的菜、不推荐的菜，一句点评。上述条目可以全写，可以只写其中几项，甚至一项都不写。你觉得别人会想知道的事，比如排队要多久、适合几个人去，也可以加进去。要不要打分、如何打分，也完全由你决定。关于篇幅，不做要求，两到三行或整整一页A4纸都是Acceptable的；
  希望保留的内容只有两项：店名，（以及如果是线下店的话）在哪里。其他按你喜欢的方式写。
  唯一的硬性要求来自 Food Integrity：**吃过再写**。

如果你不清楚写什么，我们列出了一部分推荐项（欢迎 Contributing）：
- 味道、价格、份量
- 出餐速度、排队情况
- 适合一个人吃还是多人聚餐
- 复购欲望（下周还想不想再去）
- 下雨天好不好走、营业到几点
- 任何你觉得别人会想知道的事

#### 如何添加新内容

任何人都可以添加推荐。请按以下步骤进行：
1. **撰写。** 按"如何写一条 Qualified 的推荐"一节撰写，打印或手写均可。
2. 署名（optional）。 在推荐末尾写上推荐人和日期（年-月-日）。
3. RoadMap（optional）。如果可以的话，appreciated 你的手绘路线图。
4. **插入。** 将新推荐放在"推荐列表"最后一条之后、本页之前。不要改动已有推荐的顺序和编号。

##### 修改已有推荐

- **原推荐有事实性错误**（地址、价格等写错）：在原处直接订正，并注明。
- **对同一家店有不同看法**：另写一条新推荐，编号照常递增，并注明"另见 No.X"。两条都保留。
- **店已关闭或不再推荐**：在原推荐上标注"已停止推荐"、日期和原因。不删除，不回收编号。
